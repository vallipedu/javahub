General Guidelines for Students Facing Interviews

  • Understand Core Concepts: Revise Java basics, OOP principles, collections, and exception handling.
  • Practice Coding Daily: Solve problems on arrays, strings, and logic building.
  • Be Clear in Communication: Explain your thought process logically and confidently.
  • Review Your Projects: Be ready to discuss design, challenges, and what you learned.
  • Mock Interviews: Practice both technical and HR questions.
  • Stay Calm: It’s okay to say “I’m not sure, but here’s how I’d approach it.”
  • Dress Professionally & Be Punctual: First impressions matter.

Remember, interviews are conversations — not exams. Be curious, confident, and honest.
