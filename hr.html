Infosys HR Round for Freshers

1️⃣ Tell me about yourself
Keep it professional and concise — include your education, Java skills, project experience, and enthusiasm to join Infosys. Avoid unnecessary personal details.
2️⃣ Why do you want to join Infosys?
Show that you’ve researched Infosys. Mention its world-class training (Mysore), excellent culture, and focus on innovation. Express how it aligns with your career goals.
3️⃣ Where do you see yourself in five years?
Highlight growth within Infosys. Example: “In five years, I aim to become a Subject Matter Expert in Java, leading projects and mentoring juniors.”
4️⃣ Are you willing to relocate or work in shifts?
Always say “Yes” confidently. Example: “I’m open to relocation and flexible with any shift, including rotational or night shifts, as per project requirements.”
5️⃣ Do you plan to pursue higher studies soon?
Reassure stability: “My immediate goal is to build a strong technical foundation and contribute to Infosys. I have no plans for higher studies in the near term.”
6️⃣ What are your strengths and weaknesses?
Strengths: Quick learner, disciplined, team player. Weakness: “I tend to over-analyze, but I’m improving by focusing on priorities.”
7️⃣ How do you handle pressure or deadlines?
Use the STAR method — Situation, Task, Action, Result. Example: “During my final project, I divided tasks into modules and prioritized deliverables to meet the deadline calmly.”
💡 What do you know about Infosys?
Infosys is a global leader in digital services and consulting, headquartered in Bengaluru. It offers IT, Cloud (Cobalt), AI (Topaz), and Cybersecurity services. Known for its training at Mysore and ethical work culture.
✨ Final Tips for HR Round
Be confident, polite, and natural. Don’t memorize — speak with clarity and enthusiasm. Smile, stay composed, and show your eagerness to learn.
💡 Remember: Infosys HR isn’t testing your textbook knowledge — they’re evaluating your attitude, communication, and readiness for a professional environment.
